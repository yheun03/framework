기본 아코디언 콘텐츠입니다.

single

Panel 2 Content
Panel 3 Content
multiple

Panel 2 Content
Panel 3 Content
initialOpen = "none"

Initial Content 2
Initial Content 3
initialOpen = "first"

Initial Content 2

initialOpen = "all"

Initial Content 2
Initial Content 3
